# Code of conduct

Elio has adopted the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/) as its code of conduct. It applies to everyone who takes part in the project: in issues, pull requests, discussions and reviews, and wherever someone speaks for the project.

## In short

- Be kind and patient, and assume good intent. Many people here write in a language that is not their first.
- Criticize code and ideas, not people. Accept that a decision can go against you.
- No harassment, insults, sexual language or imagery, personal or political attacks, and no publishing anyone's private information without their permission.
- Help those who are new to the project.

The full text of the Contributor Covenant, at the link above, is what applies. This summary does not replace it.

## Reporting

If someone's behavior troubles you, tell the maintainers privately:

- Use **Report a vulnerability** in the repository's [Security tab](https://github.com/localtonet/elio/security). It opens a private conversation with the maintainers. It is made for security reports, so start the title with "Code of conduct" to say what it is about.
- Or write to one of the [maintainers](https://github.com/orgs/localtonet/people) directly.

The maintainers read every report and keep it as private as the person who sent it wishes. What they do about it follows the Contributor Covenant's enforcement guidelines, from a private word to a ban from the project.
